FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Something else? DM me on Instagram.

I can’t do a single pull-up. Is this for me?

Yes. Every exercise in my Club has two levels: L1 is for 0 pull-ups or fewer than 8 push-ups, L2 for 1–5 pull-ups and 8–20 push-ups. You start where you actually are, and my tracker tells you when to move up.

Do I need a gym or equipment?

No. You can train at home, in a park or in a gym. A pull-up bar and the floor are enough for my program.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Join my Calisthenics Flow Club on Patreon and get a full Club week free — program, all 3 workouts, the breakdown, the Q&A and the tracker. After the trial it’s $10 USD a month, and you can cancel anytime from your Patreon account.

Club or a one-time program — which should I pick?

Want a single plan you keep forever? Grab one of my one-time programs or my Muscle-Up Guide Book. Want a new structured week every Monday, technique breakdowns and answers to your questions from me? Join the Club.

How do I get 1:1 coaching?

Message me on Instagram (@calistenics_flow) or email calistenicsflow@gmail.com with your goal, your current level and how many days you can train. I’ll reply with the options.

Is this medical advice?

No — it’s general training education. If something produces sharp pain, numbness, or pain that lasts into the next day and gets worse, stop and see a qualified health professional.
